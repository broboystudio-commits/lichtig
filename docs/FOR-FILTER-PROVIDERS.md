# Lichtig: information for kosher internet filter providers

Lichtig is a curated, family-safe video site for the heimish community. We'd like Lichtig to be approved on your filter, and we're happy to make changes to meet your guidelines.

## How content is controlled

- **Curated videos only.** Every video is added by the Lichtig admin and tagged (kids, music, women appear, women singing, news). Nothing is pulled in automatically unless it comes from a channel the admin has approved, and new uploads from untrusted channels wait for manual review.
- **Community shorts are moderated.** New creators' shorts are reviewed before they appear. Viewers can report any short or video, and reports go to the admin.
- **Women are hidden by default.** Videos that show women or include women singing are hidden unless a signed-in user turns them on. Parental controls can lock this off.
- **Parental controls (PIN-locked).** Options are Kids mode, no music (or no music only during Sefirah and the Three Weeks), hide news, hide shorts, hide the Statuses link, and a daily time limit. The lock stays on the device even after signing out.
- **Closed on Shabbos and Yom Tov.** The whole site closes from candle lighting until havdalah.
- **Ads** are only those uploaded by the Lichtig admin, and are never shown over videos.

## Domains Lichtig uses

| Domain | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `lichtig.onrender.com` (or Lichtig's own domain, once set up) | The site, its API, and all Lichtig-hosted shorts and videos |
| `fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com` | Fonts |
| `www.youtube-nocookie.com`, `www.youtube.com`, `i.ytimg.com`, `*.googlevideo.com` | **Only** for curated YouTube videos, played in YouTube's official player |

If your filter blocks YouTube, Lichtig keeps working: YouTube videos show a "can't play on this device" message, and Lichtig's own shorts and videos still play. If your filter can allow specific YouTube videos or embeds on approved sites, every YouTube video on Lichtig is hand-picked and listed on the site.
